Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Healing Hands (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information in connection with the healing, wellness, and bodywork services we provide, including touch-based services and related client care offerings.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, such as:

  • Name, address, phone number, and email address
  • Health history, wellness goals, and relevant medical information
  • Appointment details, session notes, and service preferences
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communication records and feedback

We may also collect limited information automatically when you visit our website, book a service, or communicate with us, including browser type, device information, IP address, and usage data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to provide and improve our healing services, schedule appointments, communicate about sessions, maintain client records, process payments, and comply with applicable laws.

In some cases, we may use your information to personalize care, respond to questions, provide wellness recommendations, and maintain the quality and safety of our services.

3. Touch-Based Services and Client Confidentiality

Because we provide touch-based healing services, we may collect and retain personal and health-related information necessary to provide safe and effective care. This information is treated with the utmost care and is used only for the purposes of client care, service planning, and compliance with professional and legal obligations.

We do not use client session details for marketing or promotional purposes without your explicit consent.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our business, such as scheduling software, payment processors, secure storage providers, and professional advisors. These parties are required to protect your information and use it only as authorized by us.

We may also disclose personal information when required by law, court orders, or to protect the safety and rights of our clients or business.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide services, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain appropriate business records.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information. You may also contact us to request information about how we process your data.

8. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience, understand how visitors interact with our site, and measure effectiveness. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 18 unless specifically applicable and lawful in the relevant jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors without appropriate consent and safeguards.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
